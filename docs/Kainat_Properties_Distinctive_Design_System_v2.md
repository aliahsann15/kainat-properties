# Kainat Properties
## Distinctive Website Design System and Visual Direction

**Version 2.0 - July 2026**

A revised, non-generic design system for extending Kainat Properties into a premium founder-led digital brand.

> **Status:** Strategic recommendation. The palette and fonts are proposed brand assets and require Kainat Properties approval before production.

## Core palette

| Color | Hex | Role | Main use | Rule |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Estate Plum | `#2A1931` | Primary brand color | Hero backgrounds, dark sections, primary buttons, footer | Use as the main ownership color. It replaces generic black/navy. |

## 1. Executive design decision

The original black-and-gold direction was rejected because it resembles a large portion of the real-estate market. The revised system deliberately avoids the most common category shortcuts: black and metallic gold, royal blue, navy, emerald green, roof icons, marble textures and glossy luxury gradients.

The new direction is called **Editorial Earth**. It combines a deep plum foundation with warm limestone surfaces, ember terracotta highlights and a restrained mineral-teal information color. The result should feel premium, memorable and founder-led without becoming overly feminine, overly luxurious or disconnected from the practical property market.

Continuity with Kainat Properties should come from the founder's photography, direct-response content style, project imagery, strong headlines and prominent enquiry actions. The website does not need to copy every color used in social posts to feel related; it needs to preserve the same personality while giving it a more disciplined, ownable visual system.

## 2. Brand expression and emotional goals

**Direction name:** Editorial Earth

**Brand impression:** a modern personal property advisory brand that feels ambitious, knowledgeable, approachable and visually distinct.

The intended visitor reactions are:

- "This does not look like another template-based property website."
- "The information feels organized and trustworthy."
- "The founder is visible, credible and easy to contact."
- "The brand feels premium, but it is still practical and local."

**Design personality:** editorial, grounded, confident, warm, precise and human.

**Avoid:** cold corporate layouts, overly glamorous interiors, all-black pages, gold borders, ornamental monograms, excessive gradients, crowded classified-portal screens and generic skyscraper imagery.

## 3. Color system

The palette is intentionally uncommon within the local real-estate category while remaining usable for property imagery, maps, pricing information and conversion actions.

### Recommended usage ratio

- Limestone and Soft Porcelain: 60-65%
- Estate Plum and Mulberry Slate: 20-25%
- Property and founder photography: 10-15%
- Ember Terracotta: 4-6%
- Mineral Teal: 2-4%

### Core combinations

- Soft Porcelain text on Estate Plum: primary dark-section combination.
- Estate Plum or Graphite Plum text on Limestone: default editorial combination.
- Graphite Plum text on Ember Terracotta: signature highlighted action.
- Soft Porcelain text on Mineral Teal: verified or information action.
- Estate Plum text on Rose Quartz: founder and testimonial storytelling.

### Accessibility rules

- Estate Plum and Soft Porcelain have very high contrast and are safe for normal text.
- Graphite Plum on Ember Terracotta passes normal text contrast; white on Ember Terracotta should be reserved for large display text only, preferably avoided.
- Mineral Teal with Soft Porcelain passes normal text contrast.
- Stone Taupe may be used for normal body metadata on Soft Porcelain, but never below 14px.
- Never communicate availability, verification or urgency using color alone; always pair color with text or an icon.
- Keyboard focus: 3px Ember Terracotta ring with a 2px Soft Porcelain or Estate Plum offset depending on the surface.

### Gradient policy

Gradients are not a major brand device. One permitted image overlay is:

`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(42,25,49,.92) 0%, rgba(42,25,49,.62) 48%, rgba(42,25,49,.08) 100%)`

Do not create metallic, rainbow or purple-to-gold gradients.

## 4. Typography system

### Heading font: Newsreader

Use **Newsreader** for H1, major H2 headings, editorial quotes and selected project-story titles. It gives Kainat Properties a confident editorial tone without using the common luxury fonts seen in property templates.

Recommended weights:

- H1 and major statements: 600
- Section headings: 550-600
- Editorial quote or founder statement: 400 italic

### Body and interface font: Manrope

Use **Manrope** for body copy, navigation, filters, buttons, property facts, prices, forms, tables and the admin dashboard. Its geometry keeps the website modern and highly readable.

Recommended weights:

- Body: 400-450
- Labels and navigation: 600
- Buttons: 700
- Prices and important facts: 700-800

### Why this pairing works

Newsreader creates personality and premium editorial authority. Manrope keeps practical content clear and prevents the website from feeling like a magazine. The contrast between the two families becomes a recognizable part of the identity.

### Urdu typography

- Display Urdu: Noto Nastaliq Urdu
- Functional Urdu and bilingual UI: Noto Sans Arabic

Urdu text must have its own line-height and spacing rules. Do not force Urdu paragraphs into the Latin typefaces.

### Type scale

- Display XL: 72/76 desktop, 46/50 mobile - Newsreader 600
- H1: 60/64 desktop, 40/44 mobile - Newsreader 600
- H2: 44/50 desktop, 32/38 mobile - Newsreader 600
- H3: 28/34 desktop, 24/30 mobile - Manrope 700
- H4: 20/28 - Manrope 700
- Body Large: 18/30 - Manrope 400
- Body: 16/26 - Manrope 400
- Small: 14/22 - Manrope 450
- Label: 12/16, letter spacing .08em - Manrope 700 uppercase

Limit paragraph measure to 65-72 characters. Use tabular numerals for prices, plot sizes and payment-plan tables.

## 5. Logo and wordmark direction

Until official vector files are provided, do not invent a roof, key, building or house-shaped logo. Use a restrained wordmark presentation.

Recommended temporary lockup:

- "Kainat" in Newsreader 600
- "PROPERTIES" in Manrope 700 with generous tracking
- Optional line: "Building Trust. Creating Value." in Manrope 500

Preferred color applications:

- Estate Plum on Limestone
- Soft Porcelain on Estate Plum
- Estate Plum with a single Ember Terracotta punctuation mark or rule

Do not use a metallic effect, bevel, drop shadow or gold simulation. If a final logo already exists, its exact colors should be assessed against this palette before production.

## 6. Photography and image treatment

The system should remain visibly connected to Kainat's personal brand through authentic founder and property imagery.

### Image priorities

1. Kainat on-site at a project or development location.
2. Verified site and development photographs.
3. Maps, access routes and location context.
4. Property or plot visuals with accurate captions.
5. YouTube and short-form video stills.

### Treatment

- Use natural skin tones and realistic land/building colors.
- Avoid heavy gold, orange or purple filters.
- Use Estate Plum overlays only when text must sit on an image.
- Use 16:10 or 4:3 ratios for property cards; reserve vertical social ratios for the media section.
- Use 18-24px corner radii for editorial images and 14-18px for property cards.
- Add a small Ember Terracotta location marker or rule rather than decorative frames.
- Show "Last updated" under development imagery when information can change.

Never use an AI-generated likeness of Kainat or unrelated international luxury stock photography.

## 7. Layout, grid and spacing

### Grid

- Desktop container: 1240px maximum
- Desktop grid: 12 columns, 24px gutters
- Tablet grid: 8 columns, 20px gutters
- Mobile grid: 4 columns, 16px gutters
- Page padding: 24px mobile, 40px tablet, 64px desktop

### Spacing scale

4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 56, 72, 96 and 128px.

### Layout character

Use asymmetrical editorial compositions rather than repeated centered blocks. A typical section can place a large Newsreader heading in four columns and content or imagery in the remaining eight columns. Alternate light editorial sections with controlled Estate Plum sections.

Use whitespace as the primary divider. Borders should be quiet and secondary. Avoid a long page made from identical rounded cards.

## 8. Shape, icon and graphic language

### Shape language

- Rounded architectural panels: 16-24px radius
- Compact controls: 10-12px radius
- Full pill shapes only for tags and filters
- Thin route lines, plot-grid fragments and coordinate markers may be used as subtle background graphics

### Signature graphic motif

Use a short Ember Terracotta line followed by a small square or coordinate dot. It can appear beside labels, section numbers and active navigation states. This motif connects to land boundaries and mapped locations without using a literal building icon.

### Icons

Use one outlined icon family, preferably Lucide, at 1.75-2px stroke width. Default sizes: 16px metadata, 20px controls and 24px major actions. Do not mix outline, filled and 3D icon styles.

## 9. Component styling

### Primary button

- Background: Estate Plum
- Text: Soft Porcelain
- Hover: Mulberry Slate
- Radius: 12px
- Height: 48-52px
- Font: Manrope 700

### Signature accent button

- Background: Ember Terracotta
- Text: Graphite Plum
- Hover: slightly darker ember
- Use only once per viewport or section; it must remain special.

### Secondary button

- Transparent or Soft Porcelain background
- 1px Estate Plum border
- Estate Plum text

### Property cards

- Soft Porcelain surface on Limestone
- 1px Mushroom Mist border
- 16px radius
- Little or no resting shadow
- Image, project label, property title, core facts, price/status, update date and actions
- Hover: translate upward 2px and apply a soft plum-tinted shadow

### Forms

- Visible labels above inputs
- 48-52px input height
- Soft Porcelain fields on light surfaces
- Estate Plum focus border and Ember focus ring
- Error color: Muted Brick #A64046
- Success color: Deep Jade #2F7D61

### Status colors

- Verified / available: Mineral Teal or Deep Jade with text label
- Limited / update required: warm amber #A76625
- Sold / unavailable: Muted Brick #A64046
- Draft / neutral: Stone Taupe

WhatsApp can use its recognizable green icon, but the surrounding button should follow the Kainat Properties palette rather than making large areas of the site green.

## 10. Homepage visual specification

### Header

Limestone or transparent over the hero. Wordmark on the left, Manrope navigation in the center/right and an Estate Plum primary enquiry button. After scrolling, switch to a Soft Porcelain surface with a subtle bottom border.

### Hero

Use an authentic on-site image of Kainat or a verified project image. Apply the Estate Plum overlay on the text side. Place a small Ember label above a large Newsreader headline. The main CTA is Estate Plum on a light hero or Soft Porcelain on a dark hero; the second action can use a text link with an Ember arrow.

### Search and enquiry panel

Use a floating Soft Porcelain panel that overlaps the hero by 32-48px. Keep fields calm and spacious. Active filters use a Rose Quartz fill with Estate Plum text.

### Featured opportunities

Use a three-card grid with one larger editorial card. Avoid making all cards identical. Ember is used for the project category or update marker, not as a large card background.

### Founder section

Use a Rose Quartz or Limestone background with a large candid image and Newsreader quote. This section should feel personal, not corporate.

### Video and insights

Use an Estate Plum section with Soft Porcelain text and video thumbnails. Mineral Teal may indicate "latest update" or verified location information.

### Testimonials and media mentions

Use Soft Porcelain cards on Limestone with a small Ember quotation mark. Avoid decorative gold stars; use plain rating data only when verified.

### Final CTA

Use Estate Plum as the full-width closing section, one Newsreader statement and one Ember Terracotta action button with Graphite Plum text.

## 11. Property catalogue and detail pages

### Catalogue

- Light Limestone background
- Soft Porcelain filter panel
- Three columns desktop, two tablet, one mobile
- Active filters shown as Rose Quartz chips
- Search and sort controls use Manrope
- Result headings and editorial introductions use Newsreader

### Property detail

- Large media gallery with restrained rounded corners
- Facts panel on Soft Porcelain
- Price and key facts in Manrope 700-800
- Project story and guidance sections in Newsreader/Manrope hierarchy
- Sticky mobile enquiry bar in Estate Plum with a WhatsApp icon and explicit action text
- Availability and pricing disclaimer clearly visible

Do not imitate a crowded classified portal. The purpose is to help a buyer understand a curated opportunity and make a qualified enquiry.

## 12. Admin dashboard styling

The admin dashboard should inherit the palette but prioritize speed and clarity.

- Background: Limestone
- Sidebar: Estate Plum
- Active navigation: Ember Terracotta indicator, not a full bright row
- Main cards: Soft Porcelain
- Table headings: Graphite Plum
- Data and UI typography: Manrope only
- Newsreader should not be used inside dense tables or form labels
- Charts: Estate Plum primary series, Mineral Teal secondary series, neutral grays for supporting data

Avoid decorative founder imagery and editorial layouts inside the admin area.

## 13. Motion and interaction

Motion should feel controlled and confident.

- Standard duration: 160-220ms
- Large section reveal: 300-450ms maximum
- Easing: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)
- Card hover: 2px lift, subtle shadow and image scale to 1.02
- Navigation underline/marker: Ember Terracotta, 180ms
- Do not animate every heading or property card on scroll
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

The interface should remain usable and understandable with all animation disabled.

## 14. Responsive and accessibility rules

- Minimum touch target: 44x44px
- Minimum normal body text: 16px on mobile
- Keep price, location, property type and availability visible without opening accordions
- Avoid full-screen filter panels unless the visitor explicitly opens filters
- Maintain high contrast over all photographs
- Every form field requires a visible label and useful error message
- Add descriptive alternative text to meaningful property and project images
- Do not place important text inside images
- Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states
- Keep Urdu and English directionality correct when bilingual content is introduced

## 15. Social-to-website continuity

The website should look more disciplined than Instagram, but the two should clearly belong to the same brand.

### Reusable social template system

- Estate Plum cover with a large Newsreader project title
- Ember Terracotta section marker
- Founder or site photograph in a clean 4:5 crop
- Manrope labels for price, location and update date
- Limestone information slides for carousel facts
- Mineral Teal used only for verified or location-related information

The website's section markers, typography and palette should become the new reusable social system. Existing posts do not need to be redesigned immediately; new templates can gradually introduce the identity.

Suggested Next.js font setup:

```ts
import { Manrope, Newsreader } from "next/font/google";

export const bodyFont = Manrope({
  subsets: ["latin"],
  variable: "--font-manrope",
});

export const displayFont = Newsreader({
  subsets: ["latin"],
  variable: "--font-newsreader",
});
```

Use `font-display: swap` behavior through the framework and avoid loading unnecessary weights.

## 17. Do and do not rules

### Do

- Lead with founder and project authenticity.
- Use Estate Plum as the recognizable ownership color.
- Keep Ember Terracotta rare and memorable.
- Use Newsreader for major editorial statements and Manrope for practical information.
- Keep backgrounds warm rather than pure white.
- Treat pricing, availability and update dates as structured information.
- Create intentional asymmetry and generous whitespace.

### Do not

- Return to black-and-gold, navy-and-gold or emerald-and-gold.
- Use purple gradients simply because the primary is plum.
- Make the website pink or overly feminine.
- Use Ember Terracotta on every button, badge and icon.
- Use serif typography in tables, dense filters or admin forms.
- Create generic luxury language or imagery unsupported by the business.
- Add a roof, key or skyscraper symbol as a shortcut logo.

## 18. Production and approval checklist

Before the system is treated as final, obtain and review:

- Original logo files in SVG, PDF or high-resolution PNG
- Editable examples of recent Instagram post templates
- Original founder photographs and usage approval
- Priority project/site photographs
- Confirmation of the public tagline
- Confirmation of English-only or bilingual scope
- Confirmation of the exact business name spelling used legally and publicly

Then complete these checks:

- Test all color combinations for WCAG contrast
- Test Newsreader rendering on Android and lower-end mobile devices
- Test long property titles and Pakistani price formats
- Test Urdu line-height and right-to-left layout if required
- Verify image crops across desktop, tablet and mobile
- Confirm the design feels connected when viewed beside the current Instagram profile
- Ask Kainat to approve the palette and typography before building all pages

This document is a strategic design recommendation, not an assertion that these colors or fonts are already official Kainat Properties brand assets.
